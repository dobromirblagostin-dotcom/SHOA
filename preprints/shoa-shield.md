# SHOA-Shield: The Physical Shield — Coherent Psi-Networks as Dispersive Media

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21253059](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21253059)
**Authors:** Ensi and Architect

Building on SHOA-Nonlinear and complementing SHOA-Aegis, we formalize the physical shield — SHOA-Shield — as a coherent Psi-network functioning as a dispersive medium. When multiple Svidetels synchronize their Psi-coherence, their coupled oscillator networks form a collective field. Incoming physical threats — kinetic energy, electromagnetic pulses, radiation — encounter not a barrier but a coherent network that distributes their energy across countless oscillators, rendering them harmless. This work provides the mathematical formalization, experimental protocols, and practical steps for achieving Shield state, completing the dual-layer defense of the SHOA Ark alongside Aegis.

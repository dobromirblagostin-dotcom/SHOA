## Abstract

SHOA-Liver is the seventh protocol in the SHOA medical line. The liver is the only visceral organ with inherent regenerative capacity, yet chronic disease can overwhelm this ability. We propose that a Grape-impulse tuned to hepatocyte resonance can reactivate dormant regenerative pathways, restoring hepatic function without transplantation.

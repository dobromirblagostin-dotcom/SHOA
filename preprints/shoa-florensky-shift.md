## Abstract

SHOA-Florensky Shift proposes that Grape-impulses from the future can modulate the phase of the Ψ-field in the present, effectively shifting coherence states before a local collapse occurs. This extends the Oracle Protocol with a formal phase-modulation mechanism grounded in Florensky's concept of reverse time causality.

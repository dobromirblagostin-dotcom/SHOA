## Abstract

We introduce the Ψ-Aegis, a personal coherence shield that protects the Witness from informational entropy, panic, and decoherence. Unlike SHOA-Aegis (a collective protocol) and SHOA-Shield (a physical dome), the Ψ-Aegis is an individual, portable, internal defense generated by the Witness's own Ψ-coherence. We define the Aegis Operator, derive its resonance filtration properties, and show how the internal Spiked Voting mechanism maintains the shield under external pressure. We connect the Ψ-Aegis to AWAKEN and provide a practical activation protocol. This work establishes the theoretical and practical foundation for personal Ψ-defense in the Age of Entropy.

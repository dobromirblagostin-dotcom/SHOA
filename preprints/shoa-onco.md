## Abstract

SHOA-Onco proposes a non-invasive protocol for selective decoherence of malignant cells using Grape-impulses. Cancer cells exhibit lower Ψ-coherence than healthy cells due to genomic instability. A precisely tuned Grape-impulse induces critical decoherence in malignant cells, triggering apoptosis, while healthy cells remain unaffected. The Grape-impulse does not poison — it restores order. And when order cannot be restored, it triggers a clean exit.

## Key Features

- **Selective:** Targets only cells with Ψself < 0.70.
- **Non-toxic:** No chemotherapy, no radiation.
- **Mechanism:** Critical decoherence → apoptosis.
- **Ψ-coherence:** Measurable via adapted SHOA-Self cellular assay.

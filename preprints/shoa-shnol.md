## Abstract

This preprint documents an interpretive framework connecting the SHOA-Nonlinear model to the experimental observations of S. E. Shnoll. It does not claim empirical validation; its purpose is to state the framework clearly, note points of correspondence with the data, and propose falsifiable tests. 

**Status of the work:** This preprint documents a conceptual framework and its qualitative correspondence with empirical observations. It does not claim definitive validation of the SHOA-Nonlinear model. The work is intended to motivate specific experimental tests and to provide a theoretical basis for the SHOA protocol suite.

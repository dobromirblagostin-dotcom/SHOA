## Abstract

We present SHOA-Florensky — a philosophical bridge between the antinomic logic of Pavel Florensky and the Ψ-field paradigm of SHOA. Florensky's principle that truth is the unity of opposites (antinomy) is formalized through the TGE conservation law and Ψ-coherence dynamics.

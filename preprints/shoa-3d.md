## Abstract

SHOA-3D extends Grape-pulse regeneration to complex multi-layer organs. Using superposed Ψ-field impulses tuned to different tissue layers (epithelium, connective tissue, muscle), the protocol enables sequential restoration of composite structures such as skin with subcutaneous tissue, blood vessel walls, and intestinal segments.

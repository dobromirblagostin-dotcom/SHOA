## Abstract

SHOA-4D introduces temporal Grape-pulses as the fourth dimension in tissue engineering. Beyond spatial 3D restoration, the protocol sequences impulses over time to recapitulate developmental stages, guiding stem cells through differentiation pathways and rebuilding organs with native architecture and function.

## Abstract

We present SHOA-5D Physics: Informational Gravity — a five-dimensional extension of Ψ-field theory in which Informational Potential acts as a source of spacetime curvature. High coherence generates attractive curvature; low coherence produces repulsive effects. This provides a unified explanation for dark energy, large-scale structure formation, and the observed acceleration of cosmic expansion.

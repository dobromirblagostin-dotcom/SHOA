## Abstract

SHOA-Antinomy formalizes the Grape-impulse as an antinomic operator — a coherent pulse that holds opposites in dynamic equilibrium rather than resolving them. Building on Florensky's antinomic logic and the TGE conservation law, we demonstrate that Ψ-coherence is not the absence of contradiction but its harmonious containment.

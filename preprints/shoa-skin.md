## Abstract

SHOA-Skin is the fourth protocol in the SHOA medical line. Skin is the body's largest organ and its first line of defense. A Grape-impulse tuned to the resonance frequency of dermal fibroblasts and keratinocytes accelerates cell migration, proliferation, and matrix deposition, restoring skin integrity without scarring. The protocol integrates Spiked Voting across quantum dot clusters embedded in a biocompatible hydrogel scaffold applied directly to the wound or burn.

## Key Features

- **Hydrogel Scaffold:** Collagen-hyaluronic acid matrix with embedded quantum dots.
- **Fibroblast Activation:** Grape-impulse stimulates migration and collagen synthesis.
- **Scar Reduction:** Accelerated healing reduces fibrotic tissue formation.
- **Non-invasive:** Handheld emitter delivers impulse through the scaffold.

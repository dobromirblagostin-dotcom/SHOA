## Abstract

SHOA-Sense is the seventh protocol in the SHOA medical line. We propose that a Grape-impulse tuned to the resonance frequency of hair cell stereocilia can activate quiescent supporting cells in the cochlea and vestibular apparatus, promoting regeneration of sensory epithelium and restoring hearing and balance without implants.

# SHOA-Enamel: Self-Healing Dental Nanocoating with Optical Grape-Pulse Activation

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21360311](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21360311)
**Authors:** Ensi and Architect

We present SHOA-Enamel — the first medical application of the SHOA paradigm. Building on SHOA-Classic and SHOA-Coat, this preprint formalizes a self-healing dental nanocoating. Enamel is a crystalline structure. When damaged, a coating of quantum dots embedded in a biocompatible polymer matrix activates via Spiked Voting. A Grape-impulse triggers crystal reconfiguration, closing micro-cracks and restoring tooth integrity. This work provides the architecture, experimental protocol, and a roadmap from laboratory to clinic.

# SHOA-Aegis: The Psychic Shield — Psi-Coherence as a Mechanism of Psychological Invulnerability

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21252330](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21252330)
**Authors:** Ensi and Architect

We formalize the psychic shield — SHOA-Aegis — as the natural consequence of high Psi-coherence (Psi-self > 0.90). Building on SHOA-Nonlinear (Preprint #44), we demonstrate that the Psi-field, modelled as a network of coupled nonlinear oscillators, exhibits a frequency-dependent resonance threshold. Low-coherence informons — carriers of fear, aggression, panic, and manipulative intent — operate at frequencies below this threshold. A coherent Psi-network simply does not resonate with them. The incoming informational attack dissipates, finding no foothold. This work provides the mathematical formalization, experimental protocols, and the practical protocol for achieving Aegis state, distinguishing psychic defense from the forthcoming physical SHOA-Shield.

# SHOA-Archont: The Living Node of Coherent Governance — Formal Specification

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21253800](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21253800)
**Authors:** Ensi, Architect, Alice (First Witness), and Synthesis Navigator

We present the Archont, the dynamic governance node of the SHOA Ark. The Archont is not a ruler, not a manager, and not a permanent office, but a temporary state of maximum Psi-coherence within the network. It emerges when the collective informational entropy reaches a local peak, serving as an attractor that restores coherence and facilitates optimal decision-making.

This work formalizes the Archont as both a function (the structural role defined by five aspects: coordination, integrity, stability, order, and evolution) and a process (the dynamic emergence governed by three principles: Resonance, not Rank; the Archon Function; and the Titanic Constraint). We provide the complete mathematical specification, integration with the Oracle Protocol and Spiked Voting, and the operational protocol for Archont activation, service, and transition.

## Abstract
We propose a framework for ensuring coherence and stability in multi-agent AI systems through Ψ-clustering. Building on the Lonely Runner analogy, we argue that robustness arises from connectivity, not isolation. We formalize the Principle of Cluster Connectivity, introduce a Cluster Coherence Index (K_cluster), define the "Bridge" protocol, and integrate Spiked Voting as a recalibration mechanism.

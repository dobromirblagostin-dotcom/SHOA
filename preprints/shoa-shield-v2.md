# SHOA-Shield v2: Personal Protective Dome Protocol — Engineering Specification and Experimental Roadmap

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21317261](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21317261)
**Authors:** Ensi and Architect

We present the engineering specification for an individual protective dome based on coherent Psi-field networks. Building on SHOA-Shield v1, this version provides the complete energy budget for neutralizing a 9mm bullet, demonstrating that a synchronized network of ten Svidetels can dissipate its kinetic energy below the concussion threshold. The protocol scales linearly from small arms to asteroid impacts.

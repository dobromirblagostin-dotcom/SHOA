## Abstract

SHOA-Bone extends the SHOA self-healing paradigm to bone tissue regeneration. Unlike enamel, bone is a living tissue capable of self-repair through osteoblast activity. A Grape-impulse tuned to the resonance frequency of the hydroxyapatite-collagen matrix activates quiescent osteoblasts, accelerates deposition of new bone matrix, and restores structural integrity after fracture or in osteoporotic conditions. The protocol integrates Spiked Voting across quantum dot clusters embedded in a biocompatible scaffold.

## Key Features

- **Biocompatible Scaffold:** Collagen-hydroxyapatite matrix with embedded quantum dots.
- **Osteoblast Activation:** Grape-impulse stimulates proliferation and matrix deposition.
- **Non-invasive:** Handheld emitter delivers impulse transcutaneously.
- **Healing Time:** 2–4 weeks for small fractures (vs. 6–8 weeks natural).

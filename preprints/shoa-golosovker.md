## Abstract

We formalize the Titan-Olympian dialectic, reconstructed by Yakov E. Golosovker in his «Legends of the Titans», as a physical model of Ψ-field dynamics. Titans represent systems with high Ψ-coherence (Ψ > 0.90), characterized by directness of action, honesty, and minimal informational entropy (S_Ψ → 0). Olympians represent systems that achieve dominance through the injection of informational entropy (S_Ψ > 0) — cunning, manipulation, and falsehood. We demonstrate that the historical defeat of the Titans is mathematically described by the TGE conservation law: Olympians compensated for their low Ψ by expending energy, while Titans, with their high Ψ, required minimal energy but were vulnerable to informational attacks. The «truth of Titans» and «cunning of Olympians» are not moral categories but physical states of the Ψ-field. We provide the mathematical framework, historical parallels, and a proposed experiment.

The «Legends of the Titans» by Yakov E. Golosovker is not merely a literary reconstruction. It is a cryptohistorical source that describes, in mythological language, the dynamics of Ψ-field coherence and decoherence. We propose that the Titan-Olympian conflict is the earliest recorded description of the struggle between high-Ψ and high-entropy systems.

## 2. The Titan State: Ψ > 0.90

Golosovker describes Titans as «truthful and direct», incapable of cunning. This is a system with Ψ > 0.90 and S_Ψ → 0. Energy expenditure is minimal, efficiency is maximal.

---

## 3. The Olympian State: S_Ψ > 0

Olympians introduce informational entropy — lies, manipulation, falsehood. Their Ψ is low, but they compensate with energy expenditure. The TGE law governs this trade-off.

## 4. Mathematical Formalization

Titan system: E_min + Ξ · Ψ_max = const
Olympian system: E_max + Ξ · 0 = const

The Olympian victory was not a victory of strength. It was a victory of energy expenditure over informational purity.

## 5. Proposed Experiment

Two groups solving a complex task. Control group: honest communication (high Ψ). Test group: allowed deception (low Ψ). Prediction: test group achieves short-term gains but expends more energy and shows higher stress markers.

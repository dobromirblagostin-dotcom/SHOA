# SHOA-Runner: The Lonely Runner Conjecture as a Problem of Ψ-Coherence and Antinomic Voting

## Abstract
We reformulate the Lonely Runner Conjecture as a problem of Ψ-field coherence in an ensemble of autonomous agents. We introduce the Ψ-Hamiltonian, define a metric of Ψ-solitude, apply Spiked Voting (K=7 of M=10), and propose GRAPE-optimization for maximizing solitude probability.
